# Research register — Saudi driving learning platform

Research snapshot: 16 November 2026. This is a first verified research tranche, not an exhaustive audit of every country, authority, repository, or mobile application.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Saudi laws, regulations and official government/authority publications govern the Saudi learner track.
2. Saudi Highway Code documents provide technical road-design and traffic-control-device references. SHC 602 is the central technical reference for signs, markings, signals, special zones and work zones.
3. WHO and UN material supports evidence-based safety teaching. It is not a substitute for Saudi law.
4. Foreign government handbooks and official test portals are comparative sources only. Never import their legal rules, test scoring, fees, penalties, or exact question wording into the Saudi track.
5. Commercial applications and GitHub repositories are product-discovery sources, not legal authorities. Their question banks, illustrations, and handbooks are not copied.

### Verified Saudi legal points included in the first content tranche

- Article 1: scope includes vehicles, bicycles, pedestrians and animals on public roads.
- Article 50: the road is for everyone and users must respect one another's rights.
- Article 51: road markings, reflectors and signs are part of the legal framework and must be obeyed.
- Article 53: traffic on roads is on the right-hand side.
- Article 54: emergency vehicles have priority while performing their missions, while their drivers remain responsible for safe driving.
- Article 55: pedestrians use designated places as specified by the regulations.
- Article 56: overtaking school buses while stopped for loading or unloading is prohibited, subject to the regulations.

Do not expand these short summaries into additional legal exceptions or penalties without checking the complete current provision and its amendments.

**Important comparative finding:** Gulf countries share many broad safety themes, but details differ. For example, Kuwait's official traffic portal describes the General Department of Traffic's services and priority of police instructions; Bahrain's portal records a 2025 amendment; Dubai RTA describes its own handbook and update cycle; and Oman ROP publishes its traffic law. These cannot be collapsed into a single “GCC rule”.

## App and repository landscape review

- The public App Store listing for **Saudi Driving Licence Test** describes offline study, topic tests, mistakes review, mock tests, multiple languages and cites Saudi Traffic Law plus SHC 602. This is useful feature-discovery evidence only; the platform must independently verify every legal claim and create original questions.
- Public app-store material indicates the competitor has free basic study and a one-time paid unlock for extended practice. Do not reproduce its questions, images, explanations, UI assets or proprietary content.
- The user's accessible GitHub repositories were inventoried. The target is `norejaberahmed-design/saudi-driving-platform`; unrelated projects are not used as traffic-content sources.
- GitHub search surfaced third-party traffic-safety projects and repositories referencing SHC 602. Their metadata and public code may inform engineering approaches, but none is treated as a legal authority or copied as curriculum.

## Important publication and completeness limits

- The first tranche does not contain a complete sign-by-sign SHC 602 catalogue, a full current violation/fine database, every licensing procedure, or all international jurisdictions.
- It does not reproduce official exam questions. All newly authored questions are original practice scenarios.
- Source access/check date is not equivalent to expert approval. Lesson records do not claim specialist sign-off.
- Exact legal rules, penalties, licence eligibility, medical requirements, fees and appointment procedures need an amendment-aware review before being presented as definitive.
- A qualified Saudi traffic-safety or driver-training reviewer should approve high-risk and legal content before a public launch.
- A later phase should add a structured sign/device model with stable code, class, subgroup, official image or rights-cleared illustration, meaning, required response, hazard context, source chapter/page, review state and change history.
- Hazard-perception practice should use original locally produced scenarios, not protected foreign clips or question banks.

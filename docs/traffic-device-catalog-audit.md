# Traffic-device catalogue audit

Status: **in progress — not a complete or approved Saudi catalogue**.

## Authority and separation

- Saudi learning and examination baseline: Saudi Highway Code SHC 602 and current Saudi traffic regulations.
- United States comparison baseline: FHWA MUTCD, 11th Edition with Revision 1 (December 2025; effective March 5, 2026).
- MUTCD is used to compare classification, device families and teaching structure only. US codes, meanings and right-of-way rules must not be presented as Saudi requirements.
- Every published Saudi item must have a Saudi source, exact figure/catalogue identifier and page reference. If the exact mapping is not verified, leave the item marked as pending; do not invent a code.
- Visuals in the learning UI are original teaching illustrations, not official sign artwork. Do not reproduce protected app or handbook artwork without a clear reuse licence.

## Required record for each catalogue item

- Stable internal ID
- Country / jurisdiction (SA or US comparison)
- SHC 602 appendix or chapter and exact page / figure / sign identifier
- Official code exactly as printed (nullable until verified)
- Arabic name and English name where available
- Device category and subtype
- Meaning and driver action, written in plain Arabic
- Typical road scenario
- Original visual asset or verified reusable asset with licence
- One or more original questions, answer key and explanation
- Review status, reviewer and review date

# SHC 602 traffic-control inventory — first catalogue map

Source: Roads General Authority, Saudi Highway Code 2023, 1st Edition, SHC 602 — Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices.
Official source: https://istitlaa.scbc.gov.sa/ar/transportation/mot/saudihighwaycode/Documents/SHC%20602%20final.pdf

This file maps the structure and page ranges visible in the source document's table of contents. It is an inventory for content engineering, not a complete learner-facing sign dictionary. Each individual item still needs Arabic label verification, figure review, plain-language meaning, correct driver response, safety rationale, jurisdiction/legal classification, image-rights review and human editorial approval.

## Chapter 4 — Road markings

The inventory must include at least:
- Centre lines, lane lines, no-passing zones, prohibitory lines and edge lines (4.2.1–4.2.5, pp. 205–213).
- Raised pavement markers, hatched areas, chevrons, parking-space and curb markings (4.2.6–4.2.10, pp. 214–224).
- Traffic islands, channelising lines and median islands (4.3, pp. 225–226).
- Crosswalks, pavement words/arrows/symbols, stop lines, yield lines, auxiliary lanes, lane guidance and do-not-block-intersection markings (4.4, pp. 226–255).
- Roundabout lane, crosswalk, word, symbol and arrow markings (4.5, pp. 256–270).
- Special-zone and managed-lane markings, speed measurement/reduction/hump markings, bus/BRT/HOV/toll lanes (4.6, pp. 271–289).
- Delineators, coloured pavement, channelising devices and rumble strips (4.7–4.10, pp. 290–298).

## Chapter 5 — Signals

Include vehicle signal indications, arrows and flashing indications; pedestrian signal meanings and intervals; accessible pedestrian signals and detectors; pedestrian hybrid beacons; and emergency-vehicle signal access (5.1–5.9, pp. 299–349).

## Chapter 6 — Special zones

Include school-zone signs, markings and flashers; bicycle and pedestrian facility signs, markings and signals; managed lanes and urban light-rail crossings; rest areas and truck inspection stations; and special rural zones (6.1–6.6, pp. 350–386).

## Chapter 7 — Work zones

Include temporary regulatory/warning/information signs; diagrammatic temporary signs; cones, tubular markers, vertical panels, separators, drums, barricades and portable barriers; warning lights and arrow panels; temporary pavement markings; hand-signalling devices and flagger procedures; and one-way work-zone control (7.1–7.6, pp. 387–424).
